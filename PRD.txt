1. Customer Marketplace (Customer-Facing Features)
Search & Discovery: location-based search, filters (rating, price, availability, services, piano type), map view, instant vs request booking
Technician Profiles: bio, credentials, certifications, experience, services offered, pricing menu, availability calendar, service radius, reviews, photos/videos, FAQ
Booking System: real-time booking, service type selection, appointment time, address input, notes about piano condition, photo uploads, add-ons (pitch raise, repair, humidity system)
Pricing Transparency: standard tuning price, travel fee, additional services, estimate calculator
Payments: pay at booking, pay after service, deposits, Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit card, tips
Appointment Management: dashboard for bookings, reschedule, cancel, messaging
Service History: online record for warranty purposes & future resale
Notifications: confirmations, reminders, technician on-the-way alerts, payment receipts
Reviews: post-service ratings, written reviews, photo uploads
Job Board: customers can post what they are willing to pay for their repair/tuning and tuners can apply 
2. Piano Technician Business Tools (Core SaaS)
Business Dashboard: upcoming appointments, daily schedule, revenue summary, customer history, open invoices
Scheduling System: calendar management, availability blocks, travel buffers, recurring client reminders, route optimization, calendar sync
Payments & Billing: credit card acceptance, mark cash as paid, deposits, refunds, tips
Invoicing: automatic invoices, custom invoices, email/SMS sending, PDF downloads, payment links
Bookkeeping Tools: revenue tracking, expense tracking, mileage tracking, profit reports, tax summaries, QuickBooks/other bookkeeping software integration export
CRM: customer profiles including piano make/model, serial number, service history, tuning notes, pitch history, environment notes
Automated Reminders: 6‑month and annual tuning reminders via email/SMS with booking links
Service Reports: pitch level logs, condition notes, repair recommendations sent to customers
Estimates & Quotes: repair estimates, approval requests, convert estimate to invoice
Route Planning: map-based scheduling and smart route optimization
Mobile App: daily schedule, navigation, notes, invoices, payments, photos, voice notes
3. Marketplace Infrastructure
Payment Infrastructure: Stripe/merchant processor integration, commissions, technician payouts, instant payouts, escrow system, refund management
Technician Onboarding: identity verification, certification verification, background checks, service area setup, pricing setup, availability setup
Dispute Resolution: customer complaints, refund arbitration, support tickets
Insurance / Guarantees: optional service guarantee or damage coverage
Trust & Safety: fraud detection, review moderation, customer support
4. Growth & Retention Features
SEO Pages: city-based pages like 'Piano tuning in Boston', 'Piano tuner near me', blog content
Referral System: customer referral rewards, technician referral bonuses
Membership / Subscription Plans: e.g., annual piano care plan with priority booking and repair discounts
Technician Marketing Tools: public booking page, shareable booking links, website widgets, Google Business integration
Loyalty Programs: repeat client discounts, family/school accounts
5. Advanced Future Features
Piano Health Records: digital logbook with tuning history, repairs, pitch stability, climate data
Smart Scheduling AI: route optimization, demand-based scheduling suggestions
Dynamic Pricing: pricing based on distance, piano type, and demand
Piano Marketplace Expansion: services like piano moving, piano sales, and repair specialists
6. Monetization Model
Marketplace commission percentage share
SaaS subscription for technicians (e.g., $49-99/month) including CRM, reminders, invoicing, accounting tools, marketing tools
Add-ons like instant payouts, advanced analytics, route optimization, job requests
Marketing/Referral Revenue
7. MVP Feature Set
Customer Side: search tuners, technician profiles, booking, payments, reviews
Technician Side: calendar, booking management, payment processing, basic invoicing, customer records
Core Product Insight
The platform should function as the operating system for piano technicians, replacing tools like paper logs, Square, QuickBooks, Google Calendar, and text-based scheduling.
