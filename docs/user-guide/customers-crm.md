# Customers & CRM — Managing Your Business

Organize all customer information in one place: contacts, addresses, pianos, and service history.

## What this does

Your CRM keeps a complete record for each customer: multiple people (contacts), multiple addresses (locations), multiple pianos, billing address, and every service visit. A global search bar finds customers, contacts, pianos, and bookings by name or serial number.

---

## Customer Overview

**(Technician)**

The customer detail page is the hub for one client's entire history and relationships.

### Open a customer record

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Customers**
2. Click a customer name from the list
3. The detail page opens with tabs across the top

### View and edit basic info

The **Overview** tab shows:

- **Name** and **Phone** (click to call or text)
- **Email** (click to email)
- **Billing Address** (used on invoices; if blank, the service address is used instead)

Click **Edit** to update any field.

### Contacts Tab

A customer might have multiple people you work with: the owner, the facilities manager, the person who answers the phone, the person who pays the bills.

1. Open a customer → **Contacts** tab
2. See all contacts with name, email, phone, and role
3. A star next to a name marks them as **primary** (the main contact)
4. Click a contact to edit or delete it
5. Click **Add contact** to add another person

Each contact can have their own email and phone. When you send reminders, they go to the primary contact's email by default (but you can customize this in settings).

### Locations Tab

A customer might have pianos at multiple addresses: their home, a second property, a church building with multiple rooms.

1. Open a customer → **Locations** tab
2. See all service addresses with address, label (e.g., "Home", "Sanctuary"), and whether it's marked primary
3. Click a location to edit or delete it
4. Click **Add location** to add a new address

Each piano is attached to one location. You can have the same customer with pianos at two different addresses.

### Pianos Tab

All the pianos your customer owns that you service.

1. Open a customer → **Pianos** tab
2. See all pianos with make, model, serial number, and type
3. Click a piano to open its full history: all service records, photos, trends, and notes
4. Click **Add piano** to add a new one

When you add a piano, you fill in:

- **Make** and **Model** (e.g., "Steinway", "D-274")
- **Serial number**
- **Year** (optional)
- **Type** (Grand, Upright, Digital, Other)
- **Room location** (e.g., "Living room", "Stage left")
- **Tuning frequency** (how many months between ideal tunings; default 6)
- **Dampp-Chaser installed?** (humidity system)

After you create it, you can view its complete service timeline and add notes.

### Billing Address

A customer might want invoices to go to a different address than where you service the piano (e.g., a school's main office vs. the music room).

1. Open a customer's detail page
2. Scroll to **Billing Address**
3. Fill in or edit the address
4. Click **Save**

When you generate an invoice, if a billing address is set, it appears on the invoice. If blank, the service location address is used instead.

## Global Search

**(Technician)**

Find a customer, contact, piano, or booking in one search box.

### Search from anywhere

1. Look for the search box in the dashboard header (magnifying glass icon)
2. Type a name, email, phone, piano serial number, or booking reference
3. Results appear as you type (customers, contacts, pianos, bookings)
4. Use arrow keys to navigate results; press Enter to open one

Search is fast and works from the dashboard or any page.

## Creating a New Customer

**(Technician)**

Add a customer manually if you're not importing from a file.

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Customers**
2. Click **Add customer** or **New**
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** (required)
   - **Email** (optional but recommended for reminders)
   - **Phone** (optional)
   - **Billing Address** (optional; can be added or changed later)
4. Click **Save**

Once created, open the customer record to add contacts, locations, and pianos.
