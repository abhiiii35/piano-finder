# Insights — Business Dashboard

View key metrics about your business: revenue trends, client retention, profitability, and where your busiest work is.

## What this does

Insights pull data from your completed bookings, payments, and expenses to show you how your business is trending. Charts and tables help you spot patterns, understand seasonality, and identify clients at risk of slipping away.

---

## Accessing Insights

**(Technician)**

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Insights**
2. If you have fewer than 3 months of booking history, you will see a message: "Not enough history yet — insights fill in as you complete bookings"
3. Once you have 3+ months of completed bookings, all metrics and charts appear

---

## Understanding Your Metrics

**(Technician)**

At the top of the Insights page, you see five key stat tiles. Each shows one important metric about your business.

### Revenue (12 months)

Total income from all paid bookings over the past 12 months, including tips.

**What it shows**: How much money your business has brought in over the past year.

### Year-over-year growth (YoY)

The percentage change in revenue comparing the last 12 months to the 12 months before that.

**What it shows**: Whether your business is growing or shrinking compared to last year.
- Positive percentage (e.g., +15%) = more revenue this year
- Negative percentage (e.g., −8%) = less revenue this year
- Shown as a percentage

### Average job value

The average amount a single booking brings in (service charge + tip).

**What it shows**: Your typical booking size. Useful for pricing decisions and capacity planning.

### Repeat client rate

The percentage of your customers who have booked more than once.

**What it shows**: Client loyalty. Higher rates mean customers are coming back (good sign of satisfaction).

### Average client value

The average total amount a customer has paid you across all their bookings.

**What it shows**: Long-term value of your customer relationships. Customers who return multiple times contribute more over time.

## Charts and Trends

**(Technician)**

Below the stat tiles, you see four charts displaying your business data over time.

### Revenue trend (12 months)

A line chart showing your monthly revenue for the past 12 months.

**What it shows**: Whether your revenue is steady, growing, or declining month to month. Helps you spot seasonal patterns and unexpected dips.

### Revenue by service type

A bar chart or table showing how much revenue comes from each service you offer (e.g., Standard Tuning, Pitch Raise, Repair).

**What it shows**: Which services are your most profitable. Useful for deciding which services to promote or expand.

### Seasonality (busiest month)

A chart showing which months are typically busiest and which are slower.

**What it shows**: When demand peaks in your area. Helps you plan marketing, time off, and pricing adjustments.

### Profit margin trend

A line chart showing your profit (revenue − expenses) as a percentage of revenue over time.

**What it shows**: Whether your business is becoming more or less profitable. A rising line means you are keeping more of what you earn; a falling line means expenses are growing faster than revenue.

## Revenue by City

**(Technician)**

A table showing total revenue broken down by city where you serve customers.

**Columns:**
- **City** — Service location
- **Bookings** — Number of jobs in that city
- **Revenue** — Total income from that city

**What it shows**: Your geographic revenue distribution. Helps you understand which markets are strongest and where you might focus marketing efforts.

---

## Clients Slipping Away

**(Technician)**

A table showing customers who are overdue for their next tuning based on their piano's usual frequency.

**Columns:**
- **Customer** — Customer name
- **Last completed** — Date of their last service
- **Months overdue** — How long past their next due date

**What it shows**: Customers at risk of disengaging. If a customer has a 6-month tuning frequency and it has been 8 months since their last visit, they show here as 2 months overdue.

### Responding to slipping clients

1. Check the Reminders queue (**Dashboard** → **Reminders**) to send them a tune reminder
2. Or, click **View record** to open their customer profile and send them a message

Connecting with overdue clients promptly helps maintain your business and customer satisfaction.

---

## Tips for Using Insights

- **3-month minimum** — Insights need at least 3 months of completed bookings to populate. If you are new, keep checking back as your history grows.
- **Completed bookings only** — Metrics only include bookings marked as COMPLETED. Pending or cancelled bookings do not affect your numbers.
- **Paid bookings** — Revenue figures only include paid bookings (card or cash). Invoices that haven't been paid yet do not count.
- **Expenses matter** — Profit margin trends account for all expenses you log in the Finances section. Make sure to record your business expenses so margin calculations are accurate.
- **Check the Reminders queue** — The "Clients slipping away" table points you to the Reminders feature. Visit **Dashboard** → **Reminders** to send tune reminders in bulk.
