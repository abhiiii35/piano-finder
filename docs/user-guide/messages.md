# Messages — Inbox and Conversations

Send and receive messages with customers or technicians about bookings and service.

## What this does

Messages let you communicate directly within the platform. Customers and technicians message each other about bookings, reschedules, and service questions. Each conversation is organized as a thread; new messages can also trigger email notifications.

---

## Viewing Your Messages Inbox

**(Technician and Customer)**

Your inbox shows all active message threads in one place.

### Open your inbox

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Messages**
2. Threads appear in a list, ordered newest first
3. Each row shows:
   - **Other person's name** (the customer or technician you're messaging)
   - **Last message preview** (the most recent message text)
   - **Time** the message was sent
   - **Unread badge** (small red number, if you have unread messages in that thread)

### Open a conversation thread

1. Click any thread in the inbox
2. The full conversation history loads, with oldest messages at the top
3. Read all messages in the thread and compose a reply

---

## Sending and Receiving Messages

**(Technician and Customer)**

### Reply to a message

1. Open the thread
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Type your message in the reply box
4. Click **Send**
5. The message appears immediately in the thread and the other person gets a notification

### Email notification

When someone sends you a message, you receive an email. The email shows:
- Who sent the message
- A preview of the message text
- A link to reply directly from your email inbox

Click the link in the email to open the conversation in Piano Finder.

### Mark a message as read

Messages you have not opened show an unread badge on the thread. Open the thread to mark all messages as read automatically.

---

## Starting a New Conversation

**(Customer and Technician)**

### Start messaging from a technician profile

**(Customer)**

You can message a technician directly from their public profile (before or without booking).

1. Find the technician on the **Search** page or via their profile link
2. Click **Message** button on their profile
3. Compose your first message
4. Click **Send**
5. A thread is created between you and the technician

### Start messaging from a booking

**(Customer and Technician)**

When you have a booking, you can message about that specific job.

1. Open your **Bookings** page
2. Click the booking you want to discuss
3. Click **Message technician** (customer) or the message button (technician)
4. Type your message
5. Click **Send**

The message is tied to that booking, so the context is always clear to both people.

---

## Tips for Messaging

- **Keep it brief** — Use messages for quick questions. For long discussions, a phone call may be faster.
- **Share details in the message** — Include dates, times, piano types, or service requests so the other person has context without having to ask.
- **Check email** — If you don't open Piano Finder often, check your email for message notifications so you respond promptly.
